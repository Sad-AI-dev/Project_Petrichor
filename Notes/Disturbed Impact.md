# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
This log was engraved in stone and later translated by the [[FIELDTECH Image-To-Text Translator (v2.5.10b)]] program. The message was likely originally written in [[Lemurian Script]].

The log is a continuation of the [[Shattered Abodes]] log.

The text was written after [[False Son]] was created and corrupted, from the perspective of the inhabitants of [[Petrichor V]] who created [[False Son]].

The line `[The world] betrayed us.` shows that this takes place after [[Mithrix|Mitrhix's]] followers corrupted [[False Son]]. `The world` couldn't be translated and likely stands in for a larger concept such as "fate" or "the universe", as no individual had betrayed them.

The line `Our enemies hound us and covet our blood.` shows the fates of the inhabitants. They were hunted relentlessly by [[Mithrix|Mithrix's]] followers, which were likely [[Alloy Vulture|Alloy Vultures]], as shows by the claw gouges in the stone.

The line `Out tribute has gutted us.` shows that [[False Son]], after being corrupted, was not friendly towards the inhabitants. It is possible he lashed out against them, leaving the inhabitants without hope.

The final line `Where is our salvation?` shows the low morale among the inhabitants. The [[False Son]] was meant to be their new saviour after [[Providence]] was killed. With no bulwark, and their tribute corrupted, there is no salvation.
