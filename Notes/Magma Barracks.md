<span class="lore">It's hot. My suit can't regulate temperatures like these. I can see waterfalls of lava flowing from grand battlements above me feeding into lakes of magma. The giant turtles below seem quite fond of it, though the surrounding Lemurians seem to have built great steel pens to cage them in. I've caught glimpses of the tribes ferrying back and forth atop their ashen shells almost as frequently as I've seen sacrifices made to feed them. These walkways between the watch towers are calm for now, but the amount of weapons strewn about are concerning. I can hear the clanking of workshops ahead. The only way left is up from here. # <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
During their journey, [[The Survivor]] comes across the [[Magma Barracks]].
The temperature is so high that their suit cannot regulate it.

[[The Survivor]] observes the magma lakes and the [[Cremator|Cremators]] that inhabit it. Members of the [[Lemurian Family]] has constructed steel pens to cage in the [[Cremator|Cremators]]. They seem to be using them to carry them across the magma lakes. However, the [[Cremator|Cremators]] require a great number of sacrifices to keep them fed.

[[The Survivor]] mentions hearing workshops and notices a great number of weapons near the watchtowers high above the magma lakes.
Much higher still, [[The Survivor]] catches a glimpse of the battlements spiralling towards a great tree. They wonder if they can escape this place without having to use a [[Teleporter]] this time.

# <span class="lore-title-inline">General Notes</span>
The [[Magma Barracks]] lay deep below the surface of [[Petrichor V]].

![[MagmaBarracks_Miner_Droppod.png]]
The [[Miner]] was unlucky enough to land in the [[Magma Barracks]] after escaping the [[UES Contact Light]]. Their corpse can be found laying near the drop pod.
They were most likely killed by the [[Direseeker]].
