# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
The log is an internal log of a member of the [[Solus Family]] as it mines for minerals in [[Iron Auroras]].
The log takes place long before the [[Iron Alluvium]] log, as the mining operation is still ongoing. It appears that the [[Solus Family]] has already discovered glass by the time of these events.

The [[Solus Family]] member discovers a material similar to gold. It notes that the air surrounding it seems charged with something and that the material appears to be looking at them.

Malfunction of vision hardware is noted due to exposure to the material.
The [[Solus Family]] member sees a beach, and 'her'.
This is in reference to the [[Gilded Coast]] and [[Aurelionite]] respectively.

The member of the [[Solus Family]] deems the gold worthless and sends it to the incinerators, for it is not glass.
