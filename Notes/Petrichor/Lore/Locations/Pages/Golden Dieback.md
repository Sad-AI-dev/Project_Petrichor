# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
The log is a transcription of an audio recording of a conversation between the two survivors `Adams` and `Sergant Wylbies` as they deal with an attack from the [[Beetle Family]].

It appears that the survivors where headed for one of the rallypoints. Possibly [[Rallypoint Delta]], but likely some other location.

The survivors brought drones and turrets, which were to be used for the defence of their destination rallypoint. It appears that activating them somehow prevents them from being used later, either do to ammo limitations or some other technical reason.

As the survivors fight the members of the [[Beetle Family]], they notice that the [[Beetle Family]] members of coming back to life. The reason this is happening is revealed in the [[Seed of Life]] log.
According to that log, only the [[Beetle Guard|Beetle Guards]] were given those seeds, so it is likely that the survivors are fighting [[Beetle Guard|Beetle Guards]] in this log.

According to `Sergant Wylbies`, it takes a few minutes before the [[Beetle Guard|Beetle Guards]] resurrect.
