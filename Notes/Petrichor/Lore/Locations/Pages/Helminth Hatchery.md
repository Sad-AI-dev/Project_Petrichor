# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
This log describes [[Providence]] forging a Morningstar, which he intends to send to [[Mithrix]].

The weapon was forged in the [[Helminth Hatchery]], which is not only a hatchery for members of the [[Worm Family]], but also as [[Providence|Providence's]] personal forge.

The log takes place after the events of the [[A Moment, Fractured]] log, where [[Mithrix]] is banished to [[Commencement]]. It takes place before the events of the [[UES Contact Light]], as [[Providence]] is still alive.

[[Mercury Blossoms]] are established to be known for its red colour.

[[Providence]] uses a large set of strange silver tools, which line the walls of the forge. Alongside these tools are endless artifacts. These artifacts were freely given to [[Providence]] as tribute by the denizens of [[Petrichor V]]. Each artifact is intricately carved and tenderly loved.

Jars bearing [[Providence|Providence's]] likeness can be found in the room. Breaking one open reveals crackling [[Starseed]] thorns and dried [[Kifruit]] skins.
The [[Starseed]] thorns are used to shape the molten obsidian for the head of the Morningstar. One thorn for each fate changed.
It is unclear whose fates this is referring to, or why [[Providence]] would want to convey the many fates changed to [[Mithrix]].

The ceiling of the room is decorated with silver chimes.

The open windows of the room allow the [[Gilded Wurms]] to assist in the smithing process by supplying heat and later by carrying the molten obsidian up to the surface to let it cool in the moonlight.

As the obsidian cools in the moonlight, it absorbs lunar rage and loneliness. This is in reference to [[Mithrix]], who was betrayed by [[Providence]] and banished to the moon of [[Commencement]]. 
This could be interpreted that [[Mithrix|Mithrix's]] emotional state is so powerful, that it has affected and corrupted the moon itself, and by proxy its moonlight.

The rod of the Morningstar is made of silver, wrapped in dried [[Kifruit]] skin and `an eon of freedom, lost to gravity wells long ago`.

The freedom lost to gravity wells long ago is in reference to the [[Mithrix]] log, where, as a kid, [[Mithrix]] throw worms into a gravity well. [[Providence]] hated him doing this, so [[Mithrix]] made sure to do it while [[Providence]] wasn't looking. But one time he was, and he loved worms.

Thus, the eon of freedom could be referring to the life taken by [[Mithrix]] so long ago, or to the fact that [[Providence]] sealed [[Mithrix]] on the moon of [[Commencement]]. The result of their differences that started so long ago, when [[Mithrix]] cast a worm into a gravity well.

The final Morningstar is sealed with a golden T emblem. It is unclear why the material or emblem was chosen.

[[Mithrix]] is never seen with a Morningstar during gameplay, nor can it be found on [[Commencement]]. It is a popular theory that [[Mithrix]] rejected the gift, breaking the Morningstar, corrupting the head and turning it into [[Purity]]. Which he then goes on to cast upon [[Petrichor V]].

# <span class="lore-title-inline">General Notes</span>
[[Helminth Hatchery]] lays deep below the surface of [[Petrichor V]]. It bears a striking resemblance to the [[Magma Barracks]] and likely lays at a similar depth.

The location gets its name from serving as a hatchery for the members of the [[Worm Family]], as their young need immense heat to hatch and mature. This is established in the [[Scorch Worm]] log.

[[Providence|Providence's]] forge can be explored here during gameplay. A shattered version of his crystalline blade can be found.

The corpses of both of the [[Gilded Wurms]] can be found across the stage. Their unique skulls and horn shapes give away which is which and that the corpses belong specifically to the [[Gilded Wurms]] that served [[Providence]].
