# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
[[The Survivor]] found themselves in the [[Hive Cluster]] at some point during their journey. They mention that it's hard to tell what is and isn't organic in this region.

They find themselves hanging from a flesh-rope waiting for a group of [[Gup]] to pass by. The flesh-rope pulses with heat.

They mention a constant buzzing, which is likely caused by the [[Archer Bug|Archer Bugs]] that nest in this region.

# <span class="lore-title-inline">General Notes</span>
The [[Hive Cluster]] lays below the surface of [[Petrichor V]].

Some plant or creature seems to spreading a membrane across the floors, walls and ceilings of this location. It is unclear why or if this membrane is produced by a different creature.
