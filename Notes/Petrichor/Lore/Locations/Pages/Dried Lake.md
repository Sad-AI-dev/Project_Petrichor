# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
[[The Survivor]] travelled from the [[Desolate Forest]] to the [[Dried Lake]]. Notably, they didn't use a [[Teleporter]] to do so. The mountains described in the [[Desolate Forest]] log are still visible from [[Dried Lake]].

[[The Survivor]] describes the location as a desert filled with old wooden structures such as boardwalks and shipwrecks. They also note the presence of [[Sand Crab|Sand Crabs]].

Evidence of the [[Dried Lake]] once being filled with water can be found all over: petrified barnacles cover the wooden structures, shipwrecks litter the sandy floor.
The temperature seems quite high, as rain sizzles on contact with the sand. It is unclear what caused the water to disappear.

# <span class="lore-title-inline">General Notes</span>
Skeletons of various fish can be spotted in the walls of [[Dried Lake]].
![[DiredLake_Fisherman_Skeleton.png]]
Skeletal remains of what appears to be a [[Lemurian]] fishing can be found on this stage.
![[DriedLake_SpearMan_Statue.png]]
Statues of large creatures holding fishing spears can be spotted in the sand. This suggests that the location once held greater importance to a civilization on [[Petrichor V]].
It is unclear what creature is depicted, or if it is a distant ancestor of one of the denizens of [[Petrichor V]].
The head resembles that of an axolotl, which may link it with the appearance of [[Newt]]. It's body, use of clothing and tools, and notable inclusion of scales make it seem related to the [[Lemurian Family]].
