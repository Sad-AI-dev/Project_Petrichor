# <span class="lore-title-inline">Notes</span>
This log is a transmission that was received by a member of the [[Solus Family]].

The log mentions glass, which is established to be a physical representation of [[Fundamental Element: Soul|Soul]].

The [[Solus Family]] member finds glass, notices how it gives, breathes and inspires new thought. It later concludes that the glass speaks wrong.

The ground begins to shake, vapours appear but cannot be registered.
Something reaches out from within the glass.

The [[Solus Wing]] cuts the member of the [[Solus Family]] from [[The Collective]] to protect itself.
The member of the [[Solus Family]] is left to defend itself.

It appears that a small group has been left to fend for itself after discovering this glass. They believe they were forgotten by the [[Solus Wing]] and do not understand their purpose any longer.

It is possible that this log is recounting an encounter between a group of [[Solus Family]] members and [[Shaped Glass]]. As [[Mithrix]] attempts to corrupt the [[Solus Family]] member, the [[Solus Wing]] notices and cuts them from [[The Collective]].

# <span class="lore-title-inline">General Notes</span>
[[Iron Alluvium]] can be found on the surface of [[Petrichor V]]. It has a night time variant called [[Iron Auroras]].

The location is a desert filled with abandoned mining equipment created by the [[Solus Family]]. It is likely that the [[Iron Alluvium]] mining operation was started soon after the [[Solus Family]] arrived on [[Petrichor V]], as their original purpose was stated to be finding enough iron to repair the [[Solus Mother Unit]].

After the discovery of class and the [[Solus Heart]] taking over the role of the old [[Solus Mother Unit]], the operation was abandoned in favour of mining more glass.
